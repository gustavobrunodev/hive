# Executive summary

Use **Enterprise AI Enablement Lead**. Current role descriptions under this title explicitly combine senior individual-contributor scope with building and operationalizing AI/agent solutions across an enterprise [1]. Closely related **AI Transformation Lead** roles add the other half of the candidate's remit: changing engineering workflows, scaling adoption, establishing frameworks and governance, and connecting AI strategy to execution [2].

The candidate remains hands-on, so the description should retain the keywords **AI Engineering**, **Agentic AI**, **AI Governance**, and **Developer Enablement**. A pure **AI Engineering Specialist** title would emphasize model, pipeline, and architecture delivery [4], while underrepresenting operating-model design, training, governance, and organization-wide adoption.

# Role-title landscape

## Enterprise AI Enablement Lead

Best overall match. Jackson Healthcare describes the role as a senior individual contributor responsible for designing, building, and operationalizing AI- and agent-enabled solutions across enterprise workflows [1]. This supports use of “Lead” without implying formal people management.

## AI Transformation Lead

Strong runner-up. Hitachi Energy uses it for a role that bridges strategy and execution, drives AI adoption across engineering, improves software delivery, and establishes frameworks, best practices, and governance [2]. Accenture's “Principal Agentic AI Transformation Lead” adds hands-on agent building, scalable platforms, governance, reusable components, and cross-enterprise adoption [3].

## AI Engineering Specialist

Valid but narrower. ZS uses this title for architecture and delivery of LLM/ML pipelines, full-lifecycle engineering, technical advisory, and mentoring [4]. It matches the builder dimension, but not the full enablement and transformation mandate.

# Cross-dimension insight

The role is not just AI engineering and not just change management. Its differentiator is turning governed AI tooling and a hybrid human-agent delivery model into repeatable enterprise capability. “Enablement Lead” captures that connective responsibility; engineering terms in the description preserve technical credibility and discoverability.

# Recommendation

1. LinkedIn title: **Enterprise AI Enablement Lead**.
2. If internal-title fidelity is mandatory: **AI Specialist | Enterprise AI Enablement Lead**.
3. Avoid **Generative AI Specialist** as the primary title; it is accurate but generic and does not convey enterprise scope or leadership.
