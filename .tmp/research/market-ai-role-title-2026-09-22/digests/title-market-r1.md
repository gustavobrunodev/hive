# Digest — AI role title market fit

Accessed: 2026-09-22

## Findings

- Claim: **Enterprise AI Enablement Lead** is used for a senior individual-contributor role that designs, builds, and operationalizes AI- and agent-enabled solutions across enterprise workflows. Source: Jackson Healthcare careers; publisher: Jackson Healthcare; publication date: current posting, crawled 2026-09; confidence: medium; class: role-title.
- Claim: **AI Transformation Lead** is used for roles that bridge AI strategy and execution, drive adoption across engineering teams, create frameworks and governance, and improve software delivery through AI. Source: Hitachi Energy careers; publisher: Hitachi Energy; publication date: 2026-08-11; confidence: high; class: role-title.
- Claim: **Principal Agentic AI Transformation Lead** is used for hands-on builders who also shape scalable platforms, governance, reusable components, and enterprise adoption. Source: Accenture careers; publisher: Accenture; publication date: current posting, crawled 2026-06; confidence: high; class: role-title.
- Claim: **AI Engineering Specialist** is used for hands-on architecture and delivery of LLM/ML pipelines and complete software lifecycles, with technical advisory and mentoring. Source: ZS job posting indexed by LinkedIn; publisher: ZS/LinkedIn; publication date: 2026; confidence: medium; class: role-title.

## Decision

Recommend **Enterprise AI Enablement Lead**. It best covers the candidate's enterprise-wide operating-model design, governed AI tooling, workshops and adoption work while retaining a technical-lead interpretation. Use **AI Engineering**, **Agentic AI**, **AI Governance**, **Developer Enablement**, and **AI Transformation** in the description for search relevance.

## Alternatives considered

- **AI Engineering Specialist** — accurate for building AI systems, but understates enterprise adoption and transformation.
- **AI Transformation Lead** — strong fit for operating-model change, but less explicit about enablement and internal developer tooling.
- **Generative AI Specialist** — broad and familiar, but too generic for the scope and impact described.
